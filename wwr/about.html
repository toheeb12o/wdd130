History

Our rafting journey began with a simple dream — to share the thrill and beauty of wild rivers with adventurers from all walks of life. Founded in the late 1990s by a group of passionate outdoor enthusiasts, our company started with just a handful of rafts, a small riverside cabin, and an unwavering commitment to safety, n, and environmental stewardship.
